# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in REEO to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respecting the project’s original design, history, and attribution.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery; sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others’ private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit permission.
- Dismissing accessibility, localization, or safety concerns without engagement.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, documentation, and chat) and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this code, and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

Maintainers must not use their position to retaliate against reports or to disclose a reporter’s identity without consent, except where required by law or to protect someone from harm.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer [@abdou-da0wew](https://github.com/abdou-da0wew) through GitHub. If the report concerns the maintainer, use GitHub’s abuse-reporting channel instead.

Do not open a public issue containing personal details of a report. Include:

- what happened and where,
- links/screenshots if safe to share,
- who was involved,
- any immediate safety concern,
- whether you want to be contacted or remain anonymous.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will consider the context, severity, and pattern of behavior. Possible consequences:

1. **Correction** — a private or public explanation of the impact and a request to change the behavior.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behavior, including a period of no interaction.
3. **Temporary ban** — a temporary ban from interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban** — a permanent ban from project participation.

Immediate removal may occur for threats, doxxing, or sustained harassment.
