REEO
Circuit

A monoline Latin display face cut from the R in the REEO wordmark. Every letter is drawn the way a circuit is routed: a single 56-unit stroke, round caps and joins, and short port stubs where a terminal meets a curve. The skeleton is fixed; the angles and proportions change to keep each shape legible.

Wordmark & voice

The three letters of the wordmark set the rules: a flat terminal at every stroke end, a small square port where a stem branches, and a 56-unit line that never changes weight.

Character sets

Latin-1 and Latin Extended-A are complete, including the punctuation, currency and spacing accents used by European languages. The face is Latin-only by design; Greek, Cyrillic and Arabic are outside its scope. Figures are monospaced at 620 units so tables and schematics line up.

OpenType

Kerning is stored in GPOS and applied by every modern browser and desktop app. The slashed zero is an optional stylistic set, so tabular figures stay untouched unless you ask for it.
